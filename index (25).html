Our Love Story

What started as a simple friendship at work slowly blossomed into a love built on trust, faith, and choosing each other through every challenge. Through God's grace, our love grew stronger with every trial — and today, we begin our forever.

"When the time is right, I, the Lord, will make it happen." — Isaiah 60:22

Jerryson & Colleen
Our story began in the most unexpected place — at work. We were both service crew members, working hard every day. At first, we were just friends. We enjoyed hanging out after work, eating together with our workmates, and creating happy memories without realizing that something beautiful was growing between us.

As time passed, our friendship slowly turned into love. We found comfort, happiness, and peace in each other's company. What started as simple conversations became a relationship built on trust, respect, and genuine love.

Our journey was not always easy. Many people around us did not agree with our relationship and even tried to separate us. There were moments of doubt and pain, but we never gave up on each other. Instead, we held on tighter and chose to believe in the love God had given us.

God has been faithful throughout our journey. He gave us the strength to overcome every challenge and protected our relationship despite the opinions of others. Through His grace, our love grew stronger with every trial we faced.

Today, we stand together with grateful hearts, thankful for every blessing, every lesson, and every challenge that brought us closer. Our love story is a beautiful reminder that when God is at the center of a relationship, no obstacle is too great to overcome.

As we begin this new chapter as husband and wife, we promise to continue choosing each other every day, trusting God's plan, and building a lifetime filled with love, faith, and endless memories. And this is only the beginning of our forever.

Dress Code

Come Dressed to Celebrate

Formal Attire

We'd love for our guests to dress in their finest formal wear. Gentlemen are invited to wear suits or barong in dark tones such as black or deep navy. Ladies may opt for elegant floor-length gowns or formal cocktail dresses.

Inspired by soft mauve and lavender hues, feel free to complement the celebration's color palette. Please avoid wearing white, cream, or bridal shades out of respect for the bride.

Presents & Gifts

As love is what the night is all about, your presence is one we can't celebrate without. But should you still believe that a gift is worth giving, a small envelope for our future is a delightful blessing.

Plus One

Due to venue limitations and the size of our families, we have made the thoughtful decision to keep our guest list exclusive to immediate family and close friends. We truly appreciate your understanding and respect for our wishes.
